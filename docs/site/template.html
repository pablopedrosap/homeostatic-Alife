The regulator test

150 founders with random neural controllers. Half feel their own energy, temperature and damage. The other half are yoked: they feel a random partner's body instead, so they get the same kind of signal with no information about themselves. No reward and no fitness score exist anywhere. Organisms that overheat, starve or take too much damage die. Organisms that gather enough energy split in two, passing on their genome with small mutations.

How the work is done

Pass criteria are written before the data exists

Each experiment has a pre-registration file committed before its first run: the frozen physics, the seeds, the exact statistical test and the threshold. Every seed is reported. Negative results stay in the record. Earlier analyses that turned out to be confounded are corrected in the open, not quietly replaced. Neither experiment claims anything about experience or sentience, because no experiment can test for that.
